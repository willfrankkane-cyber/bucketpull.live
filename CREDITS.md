## Sounds

| Sound | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Airhorn** (default button 2, `airhorn` in `sounds-data.js`) | "DJ airhorn sound": https://freesound.org/people/pfranzen/sounds/528807/ | pfranzen | [CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| ↳ underlying original | "Airhorn": https://freesound.org/people/jacksonacademyashmore/sounds/414208/ (pfranzen's sound is an edited version of this) | jacksonacademyashmore | CC0 1.0 Universal |

The airhorn was taken from the Freesound HQ preview of #528807 (via a CC0-labelled copy in https://github.com/pulkitxm/edith, `Packages/Edith/Sources/EdithKit/Resources/Soundboard/soundboard-airhorn.wav`, because freesound.org was not reachable from the build machine). It was converted to mono, its leading silence was trimmed (about 17 ms), it was peak-normalized to about -1 dBFS, and it was encoded as 160 kbps MP3 (1.99 s). CC0 does not require attribution, but we credit the authors anyway.

Every other sound (gunshots, triumphant horns, Vader-style breath, synth airhorn, rimshot, crowd "ooooh", sad trombone, record scratch, applause and bell ding) is an original synthesized effect made for this project with Python (numpy/scipy) and contains no sampled audio.
